# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in PatchPilot a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility, apologize for mistakes, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, sexualized conduct, insults, threats, trolling, doxxing, and publishing another person's
private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may
remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
Reports will be handled as privately as practical.
